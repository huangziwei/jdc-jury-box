EQMM: Is Virgil Tibbs based on a real-life character?
BALL: I patterned him after a good friend of mine who is a Negro. Quiet, doesn't let his abilities show, totally brilliant man. I must confess that, when I was taking piano lessons in Harlem, my teacher's name was Leroy Tibbs.
EQMM: Is this Tibbs prototype a policeman?
BALL: He's in Air Force Inteligence. Beyond that, I feel I shouldn't say any more.
