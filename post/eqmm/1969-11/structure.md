# Structure — EQMM November 1969

- **Column title:** Best Mysteries of the Month
- **Byline:** recommended by John Dickson Carr
- **Source:** Ellery Queen's Mystery Magazine
- **Date:** 1969-11
- **Pages:** 103–104
- **Subsections:** none. A "no new books worth praising" month → an essay recommending three classics in reissue.

## Books reviewed (in order)
1. *The Moonstone* — Wilkie Collins (Modern Library) — double volume with #2
2. *The Woman in White* — Wilkie Collins (Modern Library) — double volume with #1
3. *The Annotated Sherlock Holmes* — Arthur Conan Doyle, ed. William S. Baring-Gould (Clarkson N. Potter; 2 vols., boxed) — p2

## Formatting (per book-wide policy)
- **Bold** = the books Carr recommends, every mention: *The Moonstone* (×4), *The Woman in White* (×2), and the full title *The Annotated Sherlock Holmes, the Four Novels and the Fifty-six Short Stories Complete* (×1).
- *Italic* = works merely alluded to + foreign word: *Thou Art the Man* (Poe, p1), *frisson* (p1), *Silver Blaze* / *The Man with the Twisted Lip* / *The Valley of Fear* (Holmes stories, p2), *perhaps* (emphasis, p2).
- The Dorothy Parker quote on p1 is roman in quotes (not italic) — left as is.
