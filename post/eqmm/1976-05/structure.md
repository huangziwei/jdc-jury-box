# Structure — EQMM May 1976, "The Jury Box"

- **Source:** Ellery Queen's Mystery Magazine, May 1976
- **Printed pages:** 102–104 (files page-104 / page-105 / page-106 — offset +2; verified from footer crops: 104→102, 105→103, 106→104)
- **Column title:** THE JURY BOX / by JOHN DICKSON CARR (banner masthead on p104)
- **Copyright line:** © 1976 by John Dickson Carr. (strip; on p106)
- **Layout:** two-column, boxed scans.

## Non-Carr content to STRIP
- **p106:** below Carr's copyright line, four editorial "new books" capsule blurbs (distinct ALL-CAPS title format, publisher/price, one ends "—Publishers Weekly"). NOT Carr's reviews — strip all four:
  - YOUR MONEY AND YOUR WIFE — Ritchie Perry (Houghton Mifflin)
  - THE MULTIPLE MAN — Ben Bova (Bobbs-Merrill)
  - AN AGATHA CHRISTIE CHRONOLOGY — Nancy Blue Wynne (Ace Books)

## Books reviewed (4 → bold)
1. **The Left-handed Sleeper** — Ted Willis (Putnam, $7.95) — espionage; MP exposed as Russian "sleeper"; review spans p104→p105
2. **Revenge** — Noel Hynd (Dial Press, $7.95) — ex-POW hunts his Communist torturer; review on p105
3. **The Chinese Fire Drill** — Michael Wolfe (Harper & Row, $7.95) — Vietnam rescue thriller; review on p105
4. **The Strange Case of Deacon Brodie** — Forbes Bramble (Coward, McCann, Geoghegan, $8.95) — novelized life of the Edinburgh burglar-councilman; review spans p105→p106

## Title styling notes
- Reviewed titles normalized to **bold** (EQMM italicizes them — e.g. *The Strange Case of Deacon Brodie* on p105 — we override to bold per book-wide convention).
- "Monte Cristo" (p105, "Alexandre Dumas's Monte Cristo") = left roman: used as the character/man, parallel to Richard Silva in the comparison, not as a book title.
- No foreign-language phrases requiring italic on these pages.

## Publisher note
- Deacon Brodie publisher printed in THIS issue as "Coward, McCann, Geoghegan" (commas, no ampersand) — recorded as printed; cf. "Coward, McCann & Geoghegan" in 1976-01 / 1976-04.
