## Nature of the column
This month is **not a standard review column** — it is a retrospective ESSAY. Carr opens lamenting a total shortage of good new books ("And then there were none") and instead reassesses an older American mystery writer: **Willard Huntington Wright (1888–1939), "S.S. Van Dine,"** creator of Philo Vance.

→ **No books_reviewed** in metadata (essay/tribute). Per the styling convention, a column with no new title under review has **no bold titles** — every title named is an allusion, so **all italic**.
