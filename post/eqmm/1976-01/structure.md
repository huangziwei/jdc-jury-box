- **Source:** Ellery Queen's Mystery Magazine, January 1976
- **Printed pages:** 138–139 (NOTE: image files are page-140 / page-141 — a 2-page offset; printed numbers read 138 & 139 from the page footers. Metadata uses printed numbers; proofread files keep source naming.)
