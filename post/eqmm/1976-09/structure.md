## Books reviewed (4 → bold): "three thrillers... backed up by one really notable achievement, a vast and erudite compendium"

2. **The Stone Leopard** — Colin Forbes (Dutton, $7.95) — espionage, France vs. Russia; intro spans p080→p081
3. **Your Money and Your Wife** — Ritchie Perry (Houghton Mifflin, $6.95) — Philis / SR(2) spy thriller; review on p081
4. **Encyclopedia of Mystery and Detection** — Chris Steinbrunner and Otto Penzler (McGraw-Hill, $19.95) — reference compendium ("the definitive work"); review spans p081→p082
