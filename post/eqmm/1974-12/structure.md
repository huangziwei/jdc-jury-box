## Nature of the column
A review of **two new collections** (the "three volumes" = a 2-vol anthology + a shorter omnibus), together comprising seventeen reprinted novels. The two collections are the books reviewed/recommended → **bold**. Every individual novel named is one of those reprinted "classics already in print" / "old favorites" → *italic* per the convention.

## Books reviewed (→ bold)
1. **Great Stories of Mystery and Suspense** — Editors of the Reader's Digest (2 vols., Reader's Digest Association). No price ("presumably a gift edition"). 14 classics.
2. **Murder on Board** — Agatha Christie (Dodd, Mead, $7.95). The "shorter omnibus" / trilogy: three Christie novels.

## Constituent novels named (all → *italic*, classics already in print)
- p137: *The League of Frightened Men* (Rex Stout); *The Death of Don Juan* (Ellery Queen).
- p137→138: James Bond's first exploit = *Casino Royale* (Ian Fleming; "in Ian" / "Fleming's Casino Royale" spans the page break).
- p138 (anthology): *Journey into Fear* (Eric Ambler); *The Third Man* (Graham Greene); *The Franchise Affair* (Josephine Tey); *My Cousin Rachel* (Daphne Du Maurier); *Maigret and the Headless Corpse*; *The Case of the Crying Swallow* (Perry Mason).
- p138 (the Christie omnibus, each title appears twice): *The Mystery of the Blue Train*; *What Mrs. McGillicuddy Saw*; *Death in the Air*.
