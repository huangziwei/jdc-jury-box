# Structure — Harper's Magazine, 1966 (the "ten winners" list, first half of 1966)

- **Source**: Harper's Magazine
- **Date**: 1966 (year only)
- **Department**: "The New Books"
- **Column title (as printed)**: "Murder Fancier Recommends . . ." (no hyphen, capital F — differs from 1964/65 "Murder-fancier")
- **Byline**: by John Dickson Carr
- **Pages**: 3 — printed pages 84 (page-1), 86 (page-2), 88 (page-3). Carr's text is continuous across all three.

## Non-Carr content stripped
- page-1: top ProQuest citation; bottom-right italic bio note ("Mr. Carr, who chooses ten top mysteries annually…"); footer "Harper's Magazine, July 1966"; copyright.
- page-2: running header "86 THE NEW BOOKS"; copyright.
- page-3: LEFT column is a Holt/Rinehart ad (Davis Grubb *Shadow of My Brother*; Elie Wiesel *The Gates of the Forest*); RIGHT column is a DIFFERENT critic's column — "The Wife, Poor Wretch" by Eleanor Perenyi (with its own booklist + bio note). Only the MIDDLE column is Carr. Header "88 / THE NEW BOOKS"; copyright. All non-Carr stripped.

## Formatting notes
- Drop-cap initials rendered as normal capitals.
- One bold lead-in preserved: "**Mass hypnotism? Extrasensory perception? The power of thought used as a weapon to menace or kill?**" (Paper Dolls review).
- Book titles bold at first mention; referenced (non-reviewed) works italic (*The Liquidator*, *Trusted Like the Fox*, *Dover Four*, *Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde*, *grande dame*).

## Notable OCR corrections
- "two good ones" (not "ten"); "bejabers"; "detective" (not "deceptive"); "final gasp"; "dead and buried"; "connoisseur … pratfall"; "Lestrade"; "The Busy Body" (Westlake); "Trusted Like the Fox" (not "Foz"); "more than a year ago, an English writer" (dropped words restored); "Boysie"; "responsible"; "superspy tactics"; "manages tolerably".
