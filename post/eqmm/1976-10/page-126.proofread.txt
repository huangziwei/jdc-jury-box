Even new fiction kept pace. Though Mark Twain’s *Tom Sawyer* became a classic of boyhood, its plot center turns on the murder by Injun Joe, heralding menace anything but boyish. And, since Tom later acted as detective in still another murder case, with that note we can leave the review column as well as this splendid survey of the past.
