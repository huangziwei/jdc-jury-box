# Structure — Harper's Magazine, 1965 (half-yearly list, first six months of 1965)

- **Source**: Harper's Magazine
- **Date**: 1965 (year only)
- **Department**: "The New Books"
- **Column title**: "Murder-fancier Recommends . . ."
- **Byline**: by John Dickson Carr
- **Pages**: 3 — printed pages 104 (page-1), 106 (page-2), 107 (page-3). Page 105 was a full ad page with no Carr text; Carr's text flows continuously 104→106→107.

## Running headers / page numbers / non-Carr content to strip
- page-1: top ProQuest citation block ("The New Books (Book Review) Dickson Carr; John; … Harper's Magazine; Jul 1, 1965; … pg. 104"); a mid-page illustration; bottom-right italic editorial note about Carr ("Mr. Carr, well-known authority on Arthur Conan Doyle …"); bottom ProQuest/Harper's copyright lines.
- page-2: running header "106  THE NEW BOOKS"; bottom copyright lines.
- page-3: running header "THE NEW BOOKS … 107"; the right two-thirds of the page are ADS (Robert Penn Warren "Who Speaks for the Negro?" / "The Lure of Sailing" / Harper & Row) — strip; bottom copyright lines.
- Note: bottom-right editorial bio note on page-1 is editorial matter — strip it (unlike 1964 where a bio note followed the byline; here it is a boxed note, so strip).

## Formatting notes
- Each review paragraph opens with a decorative drop-cap initial — render as a normal capital letter.
- Book titles bold at first mention.
