# Structure — Harper's Magazine, 1967 (ten novels, June 1966–June 1967)

- **Source**: Harper's Magazine
- **Date**: 1967 (year only)
- **Department**: "The New Books"
- **Column title (as printed)**: "Murder Fancier Recommends" (no ellipsis; matches 1966 styling)
- **Byline**: by John Dickson Carr
- **Pages**: 3 — printed pages 90 (page-1), 91 (page-2), 92 (page-3). Continuous Carr text.
- **Format note**: this year the survey window changed to a full year (June 1966 → June 1967) instead of six months.

## Non-Carr content stripped
- page-1: top ProQuest citation; mid-text footer "Harper's Magazine, July 1967"; bottom-right bio note ("Mr. Carr, who selects and reviews ten top mysteries…"); copyright.
- page-2: running header "91 THE NEW BOOKS"; a footnote (Edgar Award note for *The King of the Rainy Country*) — its content was folded inline after that book's publication info; copyright.
- page-3: running header "92 THE NEW BOOKS"; a DIFFERENT critic's column occupies most of the page — "Final Solution" by David Caute (review of *Treblinka* by Jean-François Steiner, with its own bio note). Only the LEFT column is Carr. All Caute content + copyright stripped.
