## Nature of the column
Standard review column. Carr offers **four** books for February.

## Books reviewed (in order) — reviewed titles BOLD

2. **The Turquoise Lament** — John D. MacDonald (Lippincott, $6.95) [first Travis McGee in hardcover]
3. **Swap** — Walter Wagner (Pocket Books, $1.25)
4. **The Further Rivals of Sherlock Holmes** — ed. Hugh Greene (Pantheon, $6.95)
